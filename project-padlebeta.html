GTU Study Platform: Educational Platform

A centralized, structured academic hub curating syllabus-aligned engineering notes, subject-wise question papers, and high-yield video tutorials for Gujarat Technological University students.

Project Domain EdTech & Student Resources
Target Audience GTU Engineering Students
Core Stack HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Cloud Document Hosting
Key Content Subject Notes, Paper Solutions, Curated Lectures
Code Repository GitHub Repository ↗

1. Problem Statement & Target Audience

Engineering students affiliated with Gujarat Technological University (GTU) frequently encounter scattered learning resources. Study materials, past examination question papers, handwritten reference notes, and curated video explanations are often dispersed across unorganized Telegram channels, Google Drive links, and forums with broken downloads.

Target Audience: Undergraduates preparing for end-semester examinations, mid-terms, and laboratory vivas across various IT, Computer, and Electrical engineering semesters.

Core Features Provided:

  • Semester-Wise Resource Directory: Intuitive navigation enabling students to select their engineering discipline, semester, and specific subject in seconds.
  • Handwritten & Digital Subject Notes: High-quality, syllabus-targeted revision notes covering theoretical concepts and numerical problems.
  • Previous Years' Question Papers (PYQs): Direct access to archived university examination papers to help students identify frequent patterns.
  • Curated Video Playlists: Embedded video lectures from top technical educators organized chapter-by-chapter.

3. Platform Architecture & Delivery

1
Content Organization: JSON-based metadata schema indexing branches, semester modules, and asset URIs.
2
Client Interface: Lightweight Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript designed for minimal bandwidth consumption on mobile networks.
3
Fast Asset Delivery: Static hosting for instant page response times even during exam-day traffic spikes.

Design Principles:

  • Speed Over Complexity: Prioritized rapid loading on 4G/3G mobile devices during last-minute exam revisions.
  • Minimalist Typography: High-contrast text layout ensuring clear readability on smartphones.
  • Zero Sign-Up Barrier: Instant one-click access without requiring students to register or pass paywalls.

4. Challenges & Lessons Learned

  • Maintaining Up-to-Date Syllabus Sync: Adapting to GTU curriculum updates across subjects required a modular metadata architecture.
  • Asset Bandwidth Optimization: Compressing PDF notes and caching metadata locally on the client to avoid server transfer bottlenecks.
  • Accessibility across Screen Sizes: Optimizing table layouts and PDF viewer embeds for small Android screens.

Future Improvements

  • Adding an AI-powered semantic question search to instantly locate topics across all past papers.
  • Community contribution portal for peer-reviewed student note submissions.
  • Integrated flashcard revision quizzes for rapid formula recall.
